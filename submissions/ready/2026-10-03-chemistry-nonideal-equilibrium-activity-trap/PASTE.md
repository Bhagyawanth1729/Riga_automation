The reaction $\mathrm{2\,A(aq)\rightleftharpoons B(aq)+C(aq)}$ has an equilibrium constant $K=4.00$ at $298\,\mathrm{K}$, defined with respect to the standard state of $1\,\mathrm{M}$ ideal solution. A solution is prepared with initial concentrations $[\mathrm{A}]_0=0.600\,\mathrm{M}$, $[\mathrm{B}]_0=0.100\,\mathrm{M}$, $[\mathrm{C}]_0=0.050\,\mathrm{M}$. At the ionic strength of the equilibrium mixture, the activity coefficients are $\gamma_{\mathrm{A}}=0.50$, $\gamma_{\mathrm{B}}=0.80$, $\gamma_{\mathrm{C}}=0.60$. Compute the equilibrium concentration of $\mathrm{B}$ in $\mathrm{M}$. Report the answer to three significant figures.

## 6. Worked Solution  (one box per step; each body starts with Step N)

### Box 1
Step 1
Write the equilibrium condition in terms of activities. For the reaction $\mathrm{2\,A\rightleftharpoons B+C}$, the thermodynamic equilibrium constant is $K=(a_{\mathrm{B}}a_{\mathrm{C}})/(a_{\mathrm{A}})^2$, where $a_i=\gamma_i[i]$ and $\gamma_i$ is the activity coefficient of species $i$. Substitute the given $K=4.00$ and the activity coefficients $\gamma_{\mathrm{A}}=0.50$, $\gamma_{\mathrm{B}}=0.80$, $\gamma_{\mathrm{C}}=0.60$. Let the forward extent of reaction be $x\,\mathrm{M}$. Then at equilibrium: $[\mathrm{A}]=0.600-2x$, $[\mathrm{B}]=0.100+x$, $[\mathrm{C}]=0.050+x$. The equilibrium condition becomes
$$4.00=\frac{0.80(0.100+x)\cdot 0.60(0.050+x)}{\bigl(0.50(0.600-2x)\bigr)^2}.$$

### Box 2
Step 2
Simplify the activity-coefficient factor. The numerator coefficient is $0.80\times 0.60=0.48$. The denominator coefficient is $(0.50)^2=0.25$. The ratio is $0.48/0.25=1.92$. The equilibrium equation reduces to
$$4.00=1.92\times\frac{(0.100+x)(0.050+x)}{(0.600-2x)^2}.$$
Divide both sides by $1.92$:
$$\frac{(0.100+x)(0.050+x)}{(0.600-2x)^2}=\frac{4.00}{1.92}=2.08333\ldots$$
Expand the numerator: $0.00500+0.150x+x^2$. Expand the denominator: $(0.600-2x)^2=0.360-2.40x+4x^2$. Cross-multiply: $0.00500+0.150x+x^2=2.08333(0.360-2.40x+4x^2)=0.7500-5.000x+8.3333x^2$. Collect terms: $0=0.7450-5.150x+7.3333x^2$.

### Box 3
Step 3
Solve the quadratic $7.3333x^2-5.150x+0.7450=0$. The discriminant is $(-5.150)^2-4(7.3333)(0.7450)=26.5225-21.8666=4.6559$. The square root is $\sqrt{4.6559}=2.1578$. The two roots are $x=(5.150\pm 2.1578)/(2\times 7.3333)=(5.150\pm 2.1578)/14.6667$. The larger root $x=7.3078/14.6667=0.498\,\mathrm{M}$ makes $[\mathrm{A}]$ negative and is discarded. The physical root is $x=2.9922/14.6667=0.2040\,\mathrm{M}$. The equilibrium concentration of $\mathrm{B}$ is $[\mathrm{B}]=0.100+x=0.304\,\mathrm{M}$. Rounded to three significant figures: $0.304\,\mathrm{M}$.

Final Answer: $\boxed{0.304\ \mathrm{M}}$
