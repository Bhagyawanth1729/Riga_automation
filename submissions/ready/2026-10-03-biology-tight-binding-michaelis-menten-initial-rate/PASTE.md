## 2. Problem (Prompt)  [Write / LaTeX Preview]  — use Paste from Prompt

A monomeric hydrolase binds its substrate reversibly and turns it over irreversibly; the three elementary steps are
$$\ce{E + S ->[k_1] ES},\qquad \ce{ES ->[k_{-1}] E + S},\qquad \ce{ES ->[k_2] E + P},$$
with $k_1=2.0\times10^7\,\mathrm{M^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$, $k_{-1}=7.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, and $k_2=3.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. In a tight-binding stopped-flow experiment the mixed solution has $[E]_{\mathrm{tot}}=9.0\,\mathrm{\mu M}$ and $[S]_{\mathrm{tot}}=10.0\,\mathrm{\mu M}$; no product is present when the reaction is triggered. Treat the ES complex as being in the quasi-steady state. Report the initial rate of product formation, $v_0=\mathrm{d}[P]/\mathrm{d}t$, in $\mathrm{\mu M\,s^{-1}}$, rounded to three significant figures.

## 6. Worked Solution  (one box per step; each body starts with Step N)

### Box 1
Step 1
Let $x=[ES]$, let $[E]$ and $[S]$ denote the free concentrations, and set $K_M=\frac{k_{-1}+k_2}{k_1}$. The quasi-steady state of the complex requires
$$\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}=k_1[E][S]-(k_{-1}+k_2)x=0,$$
so $[E][S]=K_M x$. Before product accumulates, $x$ is the only bound form of either reactant, so conservation gives $[E]=[E]_{\mathrm{tot}}-x$ and $[S]=[S]_{\mathrm{tot}}-x$. The closed form $v_0=k_2[E]_{\mathrm{tot}}[S]_{\mathrm{tot}}/(K_M+[S]_{\mathrm{tot}})$ is obtained only when $x\ll[S]_{\mathrm{tot}}$; because $[E]_{\mathrm{tot}}$, $[S]_{\mathrm{tot}}$, and $K_M$ are all of the same order here, both conservation relations have to be kept.

### Box 2
Step 2
Evaluate $K_M=(7.0+3.0)/(2.0\times10^7)=5.0\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{M}=0.50\,\mathrm{\mu M}$. Substituting the two conservation relations into $[E][S]=K_M x$ gives
$$([E]_{\mathrm{tot}}-x)([S]_{\mathrm{tot}}-x)=K_M x.$$
With all concentrations in $\mathrm{\mu M}$,
$$(9.0-x)(10.0-x)=0.50x \;\Longrightarrow\; x^2-19.5x+90=0.$$
The quadratic formula gives
$$x=\frac{19.5\pm\sqrt{19.5^2-360}}{2}=\frac{19.5\pm4.5}{2},$$
that is, $x=12.0$ or $x=7.5\,\mathrm{\mu M}$. The root $x=12.0\,\mathrm{\mu M}$ exceeds $[S]_{\mathrm{tot}}=10.0\,\mathrm{\mu M}$, so the bound concentration is $x=7.5\,\mathrm{\mu M}$.

### Box 3
Step 3
No product is present at $t=0$ and P forms only in the turnover step, so $v_0=k_2 x=3.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}\times7.5\,\mathrm{\mu M}=22.5\,\mathrm{\mu M\,s^{-1}}$. The excess-substrate closed form would give $3.0\times9.0\times10.0/(0.50+10.0)=25.7\,\mathrm{\mu M\,s^{-1}}$; the difference is the substrate held as ES, which that closed form discards. Rounded to three significant figures the initial rate is $22.5\,\mathrm{\mu M\,s^{-1}}$.

Final Answer: $\boxed{22.5\,\mu\mathrm{M\,s^{-1}}}$
