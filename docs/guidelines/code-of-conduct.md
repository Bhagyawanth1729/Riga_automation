# Expert Code of Conduct

Violation can mean immediate dismissal from Riga and future Snorkel work.

- **Spam** — short, repetitive, templated, high-volume low-value items that disrupt peer workflows
- **Plagiarism** — contests, papers, other experts, copied or reworded internal content
- **Repeated rejections** — same quality failures after coaching
- **Low AHT** — completion times that say you skipped thinking
- **Frustrated expert** — hostility or negativity toward Snorkel staff or other ECs
- **Insidious expert** — recruiting or soliciting onto external platforms
- **Inappropriate expert** — unprofessional, profane, or threatening communication

Work slowly. Write original science. Be civil in `#ec-riga-reviews`.
